# Google Play release checklist

1. Create/verify Google Play Console developer account.
2. Build a signed Android App Bundle (.aab).
3. Use a unique application ID and release version.
4. Prepare app icon, feature graphic and phone screenshots.
5. Publish a real HTTPS privacy policy URL.
6. Complete Data Safety accurately based on actual SDKs/providers.
7. Complete content rating and target-audience declarations.
8. Clearly disclose the 18+ mature-romance positioning.
9. Ensure user-generated/AI content has reporting, blocking and moderation controls as needed.
10. Add account/data deletion flow if accounts are supported and required by applicable Play rules.
11. Configure production backend and HTTPS.
12. Configure AI provider server-side; never ship API keys inside the APK.
13. Complete Wave merchant onboarding and server-side payment verification.
14. Test login, chat limits, premium entitlement, payment, refunds and failure states.
15. Upload AAB to Play Console and complete review.

This checklist does not guarantee approval; Google Play policies and requirements can change.
